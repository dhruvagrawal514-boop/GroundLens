About Us

The Future of Verifiable Location Data

GroundLens was built to solve a fundamental problem: how do you trust what you see on the internet when you can't be there in person? We are a decentralized network of active Doers equipped with cryptographic capture technology, bridging the gap between digital requests and physical reality.

Whether you are auditing a remote real estate property, verifying local retail displays, or checking on weather conditions, GroundLens guarantees tamper-proof, real-time visual evidence from anywhere on the map in a matter of minutes.
